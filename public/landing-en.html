FAQ

Before you subscribe

How is the subscription calculated?

2,000 SAR per active project per month. If you have 3 projects, the subscription is 6,000 SAR per month. An invoice is issued automatically each month.

What happens if payment is late?

The system sends the invoice automatically, and if payment is late past the due date the account is suspended automatically and re-activated as soon as you pay — without losing any data.

Do I need to install software?

No. Bassir runs directly in the browser on mobile and desktop. Sign up, pay and start.

Does it support 3D models and bills of quantities?

Yes — it reads IFC/BIM models and renders them in 3D, and lets you enter bills of quantities and distribute them across floors manually or automatically.
