FAQ

Before you start

How is the price calculated?

Per active project by its size (starting from SAR 3,000/month), or a fixed annual license for large portfolios and entities. The price stays a small fraction of the project value.

Do I need to install software?

No. Bassir runs directly in the browser on mobile and desktop.

Does it support BIM models and BOQs?

Yes — it reads IFC/BIM models and renders them in 3D, and lets you enter quantities and distribute them across floors.

Is it suitable for government entities?

Yes — we offer an enterprise license with SLA, hosting, data residency, governance reports, implementation and training.
